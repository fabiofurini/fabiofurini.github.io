Teaching

I teach operations research, optimization and mathematics at Sapienza University of Rome, where since 2021 I have taught the master's course Integer Programming and Combinatorial Optimization in Management Engineering, as well as Mathematical Analysis 1.

From 2013 to 2019, at Université Paris Dauphine-PSL, I taught in French and English: linear and combinatorial optimization, optimization for data science and for finance, production management, humanitarian logistics and databases. Earlier I taught at the universities of Parma and of Modena and Reggio Emilia. I have also given PhD courses and schools in Bologna, Lille, Paris and for CIMPA.

My teaching material is a series of 14 courses, from mathematics and algorithms to optimization and modelling, in English and Italian and openly accessible.

Mathematics

Mathematical Analysis 1 ↗ EN IT

Calculus of one real variable, from the foundations to integrals. The course starts from logic, proof techniques, sets and number systems, then covers functions, sequences and limits, continuity, derivatives and curve sketching, series and integrals, with interactive graphs for the main results.

Linear Algebra ↗ EN IT

The linear algebra needed for optimization. Sums and products, vectors and matrices, matrix operations, inversion, LU factorization, eigenvalues and eigenvectors, norms and linear systems solved by Gaussian elimination. A computation lab shows every step of the methods in exact fractions.

The theory and algorithms of linear programming. Polyhedra and linear programming problems with their possible outcomes, the simplex algorithm with phase 0, duality theory and the dual simplex method, the revised simplex, the primal-dual method, sensitivity analysis and the geometry of linear programming.

Integer programming and combinatorial optimization

These courses, with MIP Modelling, form the Sapienza master's course Integer Programming and Combinatorial Optimization.

Integer Linear Programming ↗ EN IT

Integer and mixed integer linear programming and the exact methods to solve it. The LP relaxation and bounds, valid inequalities and cutting planes, the branch-and-bound and branch-and-cut algorithms, how MIP solvers work and the heuristics they use.

Lecture notes (PDF)Worked examplesExercisesSlidesInteractive appsQuizzes
Combinatorial Optimization Problems ↗ EN IT

The classical combinatorial optimization problems, one by one. Knapsack, bin packing, coloring, clique, routing, scheduling and lot sizing problems: their definitions, integer programming formulations with their properties and bounds, and the ad hoc algorithms for each problem.

Lecture notes (PDF)Worked examplesExercisesSlidesInteractive appsQuizzes
Dynamic Programming ↗ EN IT

Dynamic programming as an exact method for combinatorial problems. The principles of the method, recurrences, pseudo-code and complexity of DP algorithms for the knapsack family (0/1, unbounded, bounded, subset sum, multiple-choice, multidimensional, with setups), lot sizing, coin change and rod cutting.

Lecture notes (PDF)Worked examplesExercisesSlidesInteractive appsQuizzes
Heuristic Algorithms ↗ EN IT

Heuristic algorithms for hard combinatorial optimization problems. Primal and dual bounds and how to evaluate a heuristic, constructive heuristics, local search, metaheuristics and MIP heuristics, applied to knapsack, packing, routing, scheduling and coloring problems, with Colab notebooks.

Lecture notes (PDF)Worked examplesExercisesSlidesInteractive appsQuizzesColab notebooks
Approximation Algorithms ↗ EN IT

Algorithms with a proven guarantee on the quality of their solutions. Worst-case analysis and tight instances for knapsack, bin packing, the metric TSP and parallel machine scheduling, approximation schemes, and the limits of approximation for vertex coloring, maximum clique and the general TSP.

Optimization modelling

LP Modelling ↗ EN IT

From the description of a decision problem to a linear programming model solved with Python and Gurobi. Modelling techniques, the solver and the interpretation of the solution, and case studies: production and inventory, electric vehicle charging, Data Envelopment Analysis, the newsvendor, VaR and CVaR, arbitrage and robust regression.

MIP Modelling ↗ EN IT

Integer linear programming models for real decision problems, implemented in Python and Gurobi. What a MIP model is, relaxations and bounds, logic with binary variables and links between variables, constructive heuristics, and models of assignment, scheduling, location, covering and production planning problems.

Theory recalls and solvers for nonlinear models, and case studies: production with quadratic smoothing, supply chain and CO2, the Markowitz portfolio, pricing, advertising budget, continuous location, queues, support vector machines and least squares.

Interactive tools

Interactive tools I use and build for teaching.

Bomberino

An interactive workbench for operations research algorithms: primal and dual simplex, Gomory cutting planes and branch-and-bound on small linear and integer programs. Every pivot, bound, cut and branching step is recorded in exact fractions, and the worked exercise exports to LaTeX.
