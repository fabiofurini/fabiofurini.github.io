· The problem

Approximating a matrix with 0/1 factors

A summary in three steps: what we are looking for, three examples, the formulation.

1

What we are looking for

We are given a symmetric $n\times n$ matrix $\Gamma$ with entries $\Gamma_{ij}\ge 0$. Every nonempty subset $U\subseteq\{1,\dots,n\}$ gives a factor $X_U$: the 0/1 matrix equal to 1 in the cells $(i,j)$ with $i,j\in U$.

We look for weights $p_U\ge 0$ such that $H=\sum_U p_U X_U$ is as close as possible to $\Gamma$, summing the errors $|\Gamma_{ij}-H_{ij}|$ over the upper triangle, diagonal included.

3

The formulation

$$\begin{aligned}\Efull(\Gamma)=\min\ &\sum_{i\le j}\bigl(s^+_{ij}+s^-_{ij}\bigr)\\ \text{s.t.}\ &\sum_{U\supseteq\{i,j\}}p_U+s^+_{ij}-s^-_{ij}=\Gamma_{ij}\quad (i\le j),\qquad p,s^\pm\ge 0\end{aligned}$$

$s^+_{ij}$ = underestimate, $s^-_{ij}$ = overestimate. One variable $p_U$ per subset: $2^n-1$ in total.
