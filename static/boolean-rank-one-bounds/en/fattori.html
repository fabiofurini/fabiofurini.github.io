Boolean rank-one factors

Every subset $U$ of the vertices yields a rank-one matrix of zeros and ones. Summing these factors with nonnegative weights is the same as covering the bordered graph with cliques anchored at the root.

In this chapter: the factor $X_U$; its anchored clique $C=\{r\}\cup U$; why the error $\Efull$ coincides with Stage A of AWCD (Anchored Weighted Clique Decomposition); why Stage B measures the trace and not the number of factors.

One subset, one matrix

Definition

For $\emptyset\neq U\subseteq V$ let $\one_U$ be the indicator vector of $U$: it equals $1$ on the indices of $U$ and $0$ elsewhere. The associated factor is

$$X_U=\one_U\one_U^\top,\qquad (X_U)_{ij}=1\iff i,j\in U.$$

It is a symmetric matrix of zeros and ones, of rank one: a block of ones on the rows and columns of $U$.

In the bordered graph (Chapter A: vertices $V\cup\{r\}$, root $r$) the same $U$ determines the anchored clique $C=\{r\}\cup U$. We write $E(C)$ for the edges with both endpoints in $C$.

The vector $\chi^{E(C)}$ is the incidence vector of $E(C)$: it has one component for each edge of the bordered graph, equal to $1$ if the edge lies in $E(C)$ and to $0$ otherwise. The AWCD matrix $B$ has one column $\chi^{E(C)}$ for each anchored clique $C$.

The dictionary

Read the edge $(r,i)$ as the cell $(i,i)$ and the edge $\{i,j\}$ as the cell $(i,j)$, with $i<j$. Then the column $\chi^{E(C)}$ of $B$ is the factor $X_U$ read on the upper triangle, diagonal included.

In particular $|C|-1=|U|=\tr X_U$, where $\tr$ is the trace (the sum of the diagonal entries).

Summing the factors

With coefficients $p_U\ge 0$ we reconstruct the matrix $H(p)=\sum_U p_U X_U$. In the language of AWCD the coefficient is called $x_C$, with $x_C=p_U$ for $C=\{r\}\cup U$.

Theorem (equivalence with Stage A of AWCD)

Let $\Efull(\Gamma)$ be the full error: the minimum, over $p\ge 0$, of $\sum_{1\le i\le j\le n}|\Gamma_{ij}-H_{ij}(p)|$ (each pair counted once, diagonal included). Let $z^*$ be the value of Stage A of AWCD with the single root $r$:

$$z^*=\min\{e^\top q^++e^\top q^-:\ Bx+q^+-q^-=w,\ x,q^\pm\ge0\}.$$

Here $w$ is the weight vector of the bordered graph, $e$ the all-ones vector, $q^+$ the under-coverage and $q^-$ the over-coverage. Then $\Efull(\Gamma)=z^*$.

Proof

The anchored cliques $C=\{r\}\cup U$ are in bijection with the subsets $\emptyset\ne U\subseteq V$; set $x_C=p_U$. By the dictionary, the component of $Bx$ on the edge $(r,i)$ equals $\sum_{U\ni i}p_U=H_{ii}(p)$, and the one on the edge $\{i,j\}$ equals $\sum_{U\supseteq\{i,j\}}p_U=H_{ij}(p)$. Hence $Bx$ is the upper triangle of $H(p)$.

The constraint $Bx+q^+-q^-=w$ becomes, cell by cell, $H_{ij}(p)+q^+_{ij}-q^-_{ij}=\Gamma_{ij}$. At an optimum at most one of $q^+_{ij}$ and $q^-_{ij}$ is positive, so $q^+_{ij}+q^-_{ij}=|\Gamma_{ij}-H_{ij}(p)|$. The objectives coincide, and so do the minima. $\square$

Stage B measures the trace, not the number of factors

Among the optima of Stage A, Stage B minimizes $\sum_C(|C|-1)\,x_C=\sum_U|U|\,p_U$. Since $\tr X_U=|U|$, this sum is $\tr H$: it depends only on $H$, not on how $H$ is decomposed.

$$X_{\{1,2\}}+X_{\{1,3\}}+X_{\{2,3\}}=\begin{pmatrix}2&1&1\\1&2&1\\1&1&2\end{pmatrix}=X_{\{1,2,3\}}+X_{\{1\}}+X_{\{2\}}+X_{\{3\}}$$

Three factors on the left, four on the right: cost $2+2+2=6=3+1+1+1$ in both cases. Try the two decompositions with the buttons of the figure above.

An example where Stage B decides

For $\Gamma=\begin{pmatrix}0&1&2\\1&1&0\\2&0&1\end{pmatrix}$ (the reference example) both $H=\one\one^\top$ (the all-ones matrix, $p_{\{1,2,3\}}=1$) and $H=\mathrm{diag}(0,1,1)$ ($p_{\{2\}}=p_{\{3\}}=1$; the diagonal matrix with diagonal $0,1,1$) have error $3=\Efull(\Gamma)$. Stage B chooses the second one, with trace $2$ instead of $3$. Here too the factors are one against two: what counts is the trace.

Key takeaways
  • A subset $U$ gives the factor $X_U=\one_U\one_U^\top$ and the anchored clique $C=\{r\}\cup U$.
  • The column $\chi^{E(C)}$ of $B$ is $X_U$ read on the upper triangle; $x_C=p_U$ and $Bx$ is the upper triangle of $H(p)$.
  • Hence $\Efull(\Gamma)=z^*$: the full error is Stage A of AWCD with a single root.
  • Stage B minimizes $\sum_U|U|p_U=\tr H$, not the number of factors.
