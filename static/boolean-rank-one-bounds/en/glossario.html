≡ Glossary

All the notation in one place

Every symbol used in the guide, with its meaning. The chapters define each one where it first appears; here you can find it again when you need it.

Matrix and factors

$V=\{1,\dots,n\}$the row and column indices, i.e. the vertices of the graphs
$\Gamma\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}_{\ge 0}$the matrix to approximate: symmetric, with nonnegative entries
$\Gamma_{ii}$, $\Gamma_{ij}$ ($i<j$)diagonal and off-diagonal entries; each pair $\{i,j\}$ is counted only once
$\gamma=\sum_i\Gamma_{ii}$the trace of $\Gamma$
$\one_U$the indicator vector of $U\subseteq V$ (1 on the indices in $U$, 0 elsewhere)
$X_U=\one_U\one_U^\top$the Boolean rank-one factor of $U\neq\emptyset$: $(X_U)_{ij}=1$ if and only if $i,j\in U$
$p_U\ge 0$the coefficient of the factor $X_U$
$H=H(p)=\sum_U p_UX_U$the reconstructed matrix (the reconstruction)
$\COR(n)=\cone\{X_U\}$the correlation cone: all the matrices $H(p)$ with $p\ge 0$
$\tr H$the trace of $H$; it equals $\tr H=\sum_U|U|\,p_U$
$\one\one^\top$, $\mathrm{diag}(a)$the all-ones matrix; the diagonal matrix with diagonal $a$
$bb^\top$a rank-one matrix, with $b\in\mathbb R^n_{\ge 0}$

Errors and the full problem

underestimate$H_{ij}<\Gamma_{ij}$ (positive error); in the full problem it is measured by the slack $s^+_{ij}\ge 0$
overestimate$H_{ij}>\Gamma_{ij}$ (negative error); measured by the slack $s^-_{ij}\ge 0$, with $H_{ij}+s^+_{ij}-s^-_{ij}=\Gamma_{ij}$
$\Efull(\Gamma)$$\min_{p\ge0}\sum_{i\le j}|\Gamma_{ij}-H_{ij}(p)|$: the minimum $L^1$ error over the upper triangle, diagonal included (also called $\mathrm{OPT}_{\ell_1}(\Gamma)$)
$(x)^+$$\max\{0,x\}$, the positive part
LPlinear program (linear programming); the LP dual is its dual problem

AWCD and the bordered graph

AWCDAnchored Weighted Clique Decomposition: decomposition of the weight vector into weighted cliques that contain an anchor $S$
$r$ (also $i_0$)the root added by AWCD; the anchor is $S=\{r\}$
bordered graphthe complete graph on $V\cup\{r\}$, with weights $w_{ri}=\Gamma_{ii}$ and $w_{ij}=\Gamma_{ij}$
$\K(G,\{r\})$the family of anchored cliques $C=\{r\}\cup U$, $U\neq\emptyset$
$E(C)$, $\chi^{E(C)}$the edges of the clique $C$ and their incidence vector (the column of $C$ in the matrix $B$)
$x_C$the multiplicity of the clique $C$; it equals $x_C=p_U$
$q^+$, $q^-$under-coverage and over-coverage of the edges in Stage A
Stage A, $z^*$$z^*=\min\{e^\top q^++e^\top q^-:\ Bx+q^+-q^-=w,\ x,q^\pm\ge0\}$; it equals $z^*=\Efull(\Gamma)$
Stage B, $f_2^*$among the optima of Stage A, minimizes $\sum_C(|C|-1)x_C=\tr H$ (the trace, not the number of factors); $f_2^*$ is its value
$(D_A)$the LP dual of Stage A: $\max\{w^\top y:\ \sum_{e\in E(C)}y_e\le0\ \forall C,\ -1\le y\le1\}$
$\chi^{\{e\}}$the vector with a 1 on the edge $e$ and 0 elsewhere
$\varphi$in the matching bound: assigns to each chosen edge one of its endpoints, without repeating vertices

Relaxation, pseudoforests and certificates

$(R)$the one-sided relaxation: $\min\sum_U|U|p_U+\sum_{i<j}q_{ij}$ with coverage of the diagonal and of the pairs; the off-diagonal overestimate is free
$q_{ij}$the off-diagonal underestimate in $(R)$ (paid)
$\val(\cdot)$the optimal value of a problem
$\LB=\val(R)-\gamma$the lower bound on $\Efull(\Gamma)$; subtracting $\gamma$ is essential
$\ell_i$the loop on vertex $i$, with weight $\Gamma_{ii}$
elementsthe loops $\ell_i$ and the ordinary edges $\{i,j\}$ of the graph with loops
$E[U]$the elements with all their endpoints in $U$
$(P)$the LP dual of $(R)$: $\max\sum_i\Gamma_{ii}x_{\ell_i}+\sum_{i<j}\Gamma_{ij}x_{ij}$ with $x(E[U])\le|U|$, $x_{ij}\le1$, $x\ge0$
pseudoforesta set of elements in which every connected component has at most one cycle (a loop is a cycle)
bicircular matroidthe matroid whose independent sets are the pseudoforests
$w(F)=\sum_{e\in F}w_e$the weight of a set of elements
$F^*$a maximum-weight pseudoforest (returned by the greedy algorithm); $w(F^*)=\val(P)=\val(R)$
greedyscans the elements by nonincreasing weight and keeps each one that leaves the set independent
component peelingthe component-by-component construction that derives an optimum $(p,q)$ of $(R)$ from the pseudoforest
laminara family of sets that are pairwise disjoint or nested (the support of the peeling, at most $2n-1$ sets)
$\alpha_v=\sum_{U\ni v}p_U$the marginal of vertex $v$
$\theta_k$, $S_k$the distinct values of the marginals and the superlevel sets $S_k=\{v:\alpha_v\ge\theta_k\}$
chaina family of nested sets $S_1\supsetneq S_2\supsetneq\cdots$ (at most $n$); it covers the pair $\{i,j\}$ exactly $\min(\alpha_i,\alpha_j)$ times
$\beta_i=\alpha_i-\Gamma_{ii}$the diagonal excess in the compact formulation

Matching and the graphic matroid

transversal representationa set of elements is independent if and only if each element can be assigned to one of its endpoints (a loop to its vertex), with all vertices distinct
gain$\Gamma_{ij}-\Gamma_{vv}$: how much the weight changes when the edge $\{i,j\}$ replaces the loop of $v\in\{i,j\}$
$M$the value of the maximum-weight bipartite matching between edges and vertices, with weights $(\Gamma_{ij}-\Gamma_{vv})^+$; $M=\LB=$ AWCD matching bound
$y_{ij,i}$the matching variable that assigns the edge $\{i,j\}$ to vertex $i$
totally unimodulara matrix whose every subdeterminant lies in $\{-1,0,1\}$: the LP has integral optima
foresta set of edges without cycles (an independent set of the graphic matroid)
1-forestordinary edges with at most one cycle per component (bicircular without loops)
$T$, MSTa maximum spanning forest (MST: maximum spanning tree), computed with Kruskal's algorithm
$\Zcov(w)=w(T)$the value of the unanchored covering of AWCD; it satisfies $\Zcov\le z^*+f_2^*$ and is not a bound on the distance alone
