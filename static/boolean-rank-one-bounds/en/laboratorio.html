Laboratory: change the graph, watch the solutions change

One matrix $\Gamma$, seven structures that read it in different ways. Change a weight or load an example: forest, 1-forest, pseudoforest, forest of the bordered graph, matching and exact optimum are recomputed at once, together with the chain of bounds.

In this laboratory: see where the graphic and the bicircular matroid part ways, why $\LB=\val(R)-\gamma=w(F^*)-\gamma=M$ always holds, and when $\Efull(\Gamma)$ is strictly larger instead.
How to use it
  • Select an element with a click: an edge $\{i,j\}$, a vertex (that is, its loop $\ell_i$, of weight $\Gamma_{ii}$) or a matrix cell. This also works on the graphs of the cards.
  • Change the weight in the sticky bar at the top: slider, number field, $\pm$ buttons, mouse wheel over the selected element, or ↑/↓ arrows (with Shift: $\pm 0.1$). Weight $0$ = missing edge, drawn dashed.
  • Drag the vertices to redraw the graph; “Re-align” puts them back on the polygon.
  • Undo / redo with ↶ ↷ (or ⌘/Ctrl+Z, Shift+⌘/Ctrl+Z). “Copy link” copies the address with the state encoded in #laboratorio=….
  • In the cards, ▶ greedy opens the step-by-step replay of the algorithm; below, the tabs show bounds, formulations, certificates and a few challenges.

Exact optimum $\Efull(\Gamma)$

LP in the browser, $n\le5$

The first four bars are weights of structures (maximization), the last one is an error (minimization). It always holds: forest $\le$ 1-forest $\le\val(P)$ and forest $\le\Zcov\le\val(P)=\gamma+M$. $\Zcov$ satisfies $\Zcov(w)\le z^*+f_2^*$ (with $z^*$ and $f_2^*$): it is not a bound on the distance $z^*=\Efull(\Gamma)$ alone.

$(R)$ one-sided relaxation
$$\begin{aligned}\min\ &\textstyle\sum_{U}|U|\,p_U+\sum_{i\lt j}q_{ij}\\ \text{s.t. }&\textstyle\sum_{U\supseteq\{i,j\}}p_U+q_{ij}\ge\Gamma_{ij}\ \ (i\lt j)\\ &\textstyle\sum_{U\ni i}p_U\ge\Gamma_{ii}\ \ (i\in V),\qquad p,q\ge0\end{aligned}$$
$(P)$ pseudoforests, LP dual of $(R)$
$$\begin{aligned}\max\ &\textstyle\sum_i\Gamma_{ii}x_{\ell_i}+\sum_{i\lt j}\Gamma_{ij}x_{ij}\\ \text{s.t. }&x(E[U])\le|U|\quad\forall U\ne\emptyset\\ &x_{ij}\le1,\qquad x\ge0\end{aligned}$$
$(D_A)$ dual of AWCD Stage A
$$\begin{aligned}\max\ &\textstyle\sum_{i\le j}\Gamma_{ij}\,y_{ij}\\ \text{s.t. }&\textstyle\sum_{i\le j,\ i,j\in U}y_{ij}\le0\quad\forall U\ne\emptyset\\ &-1\le y\le1\end{aligned}$$
Matching and its dual (totally unimodular matrix)
$$\begin{aligned}M=\min\ &\textstyle\sum_i\beta_i+\sum_{i\lt j}q_{ij}\\ \text{s.t. }&\beta_i+q_{ij}\ge\Gamma_{ij}-\Gamma_{ii}\quad(i\lt j)\\ &\beta_j+q_{ij}\ge\Gamma_{ij}-\Gamma_{jj}\quad(i\lt j)\\ &\beta,q\ge0\end{aligned}$$
