Teaching

I have experience in teaching operations research, optimization and mathematics. I have taught at Sapienza University of Rome, Université Paris Dauphine-PSL, the University of Parma and the University of Modena and Reggio Emilia, in Italian, French and English: about 2,000 hours at every level, licence and bachelor's, master's and PhD.

During my teaching I have prepared the following 14 online courses, in English and Italian, openly accessible. Each course has lecture notes with definitions, theorems and proofs, worked examples and exercises with solutions, slides, interactive apps and self-assessment quizzes, and the lecture notes and slides can be downloaded in PDF.

Integer Linear Programming ↗

EN IT

Integer and mixed integer linear programming and the exact methods to solve it. The LP relaxation and bounds, valid inequalities and cutting planes, the branch-and-bound and branch-and-cut algorithms, how MIP solvers work and the heuristics they use.

Combinatorial Optimization Problems ↗

EN IT

The classical combinatorial optimization problems, one by one. Knapsack, bin packing, coloring, clique, routing, scheduling and lot sizing problems: their definitions, integer programming formulations with their properties and bounds, and the ad hoc algorithms for each problem.

Dynamic Programming ↗

EN IT

Dynamic programming as an exact method for combinatorial problems. The principles of the method, recurrences, pseudo-code and complexity of DP algorithms for the knapsack family (0/1, unbounded, bounded, subset sum, multiple-choice, multidimensional, with setups), lot sizing, coin change and rod cutting.

What a MIP model is, relaxations and bounds, logic with binary variables and links between variables, constructive heuristics, and models of assignment, scheduling, location, covering and production planning problems.

Interactive tools

Interactive tools I use and build for teaching.

Bomberino

An interactive workbench for operations research algorithms: primal and dual simplex, Gomory cutting planes and branch-and-bound on small linear and integer programs. Every pivot, bound, cut and branching step is recorded in exact fractions, and the worked exercise exports to LaTeX.
